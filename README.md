🎓 SR EduSphere

Your Degree. Your Progress. Your Future.

SR EduSphere – Engineering Academic Management System is a student-focused academic management platform designed to organize and monitor the engineering academic journey.

🌐 Live Application

"Open SR EduSphere" (https://sr-edusphere.streamlit.app/)

✨ Features

- 📊 Academic Dashboard
- 📚 Semester Management (1–8)
- 📖 Courses and Syllabus Tracking
- 🎓 University Syllabus
- 📅 Academic and Leave Calendar
- 📝 Internal Assessment Management
- 📈 Academic Status Tracker
- 🏆 Scorecard and Progress Monitoring
- ✅ Study Plan and Checklist
- 🤖 AI-Powered Academic Assistant
- 🔐 Supabase Database Integration

🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Streamlit
- Supabase
- Google Gemini AI
- Pandas

🎯 Project Objective

To provide engineering students with a centralized platform for managing academic information, tracking progress, organizing assessments, and planning their studies.
